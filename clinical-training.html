DEPARTMENT OF CLINICAL MEDICINE

Learning in practice

Connecting clinical knowledge with practical skills and professional responsibility.

Clinical learning connects classroom knowledge with practical skills and professional responsibility.

Practical education in clinical medicine can include patient assessment, clinical reasoning, communication and the application of evidence in care. Learning must be guided by patient safety, consent and professional ethics.

Practical learning

Students develop a foundation for taking histories, conducting examinations, communicating clearly and reflecting on clinical decisions. Specific skills, rotations and assessment requirements should be confirmed against the approved curriculum.

Supervision & professional ethics

Clinical learning depends on appropriate supervision, confidentiality, respect and safe practice. Students should follow university and placement-site policies and seek guidance from their supervisors.

Facilities and placements

Details of current facilities, hospital-based learning, placement arrangements and rotation schedules require departmental confirmation. Do not assume that the illustrative photograph depicts an Egerton facility.
