DEPARTMENT OF CLINICAL MEDICINE

Our department

Learn about our purpose and place within the Faculty of Health Sciences.

An academic home for clinical medicine education, research and service.

The Department of Clinical Medicine is part of Egerton University’s Faculty of Health Sciences. This website brings together information for prospective students, current students and members of the wider academic community.

Our mission

“To train high caliber clinical medicine students, carry out theory, clinical work and research in current and emerging challenges.”

Departmental mission provided for this website; official source and current wording require confirmation.

Our role in health sciences

The department’s academic focus brings together clinical knowledge, practical learning and inquiry. Its programmes sit within the university’s broader health sciences community.

Our commitment

Clinical education is grounded in respect for patients, professional integrity, evidence-informed practice and lifelong learning. These are guiding principles for this website, not a statement of officially adopted departmental values.
