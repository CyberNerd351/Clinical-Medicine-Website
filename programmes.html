DEPARTMENT OF CLINICAL MEDICINE

Study clinical medicine

Explore our listed undergraduate pathways and find out where to get current admissions information.

Two pathways in clinical medicine are listed by the university. Contact admissions for the current curriculum, eligibility and application guidance.

Source: Egerton University programme listing . The descriptions below are general guidance, not approved curricula.

01 / UNDERGRADUATE

BSc Clinical Medicine
Pre-Service

For prospective students seeking an entry pathway into clinical medicine education. Confirm eligibility directly with admissions.

Learning focus
Building foundational clinical knowledge, patient assessment skills, professional ethics and an understanding of research.
Programme structure & duration
Contact the department for the current approved structure and duration.
Admission requirements
Contact admissions for the latest requirements and application process.
Career opportunities
Clinical practice and related health-sector roles may be possible subject to applicable qualifications, registration and regulatory requirements. Confirm current pathways with the relevant regulator.

02 / UNDERGRADUATE

BSc Clinical Medicine
In-Service

An in-service pathway for prospective applicants with prior professional training or experience; eligibility must be confirmed with admissions.

Learning focus
Developing clinical knowledge, assessment, ethical decision-making and research literacy.
Programme structure & duration
Contact the department for the current approved structure and duration.
Admission requirements
Contact admissions for the latest requirements and application process.
Career opportunities
Career options depend on qualifications, professional registration and current regulations. Seek guidance from the university and relevant regulator.
